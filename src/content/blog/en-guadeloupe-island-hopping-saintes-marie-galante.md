# Island Hopping Around Guadeloupe: Les Saintes, Marie-Galante & La Désirade

Guadeloupe is not just one island, but a vibrant archipelago of five inhabited islands, each with its own distinctive historical identity and landscape.

---

## 1. Les Saintes (Terre-de-Haut & Terre-de-Bas)
- **Terre-de-Haut**: Home to the Bay of Les Saintes, recognized by UNESCO as one of the world's most beautiful bays. Explore Fort Napoléon, taste the local *Tourment d'Amour* pastry, and rent an electric scooter to visit Plage de Pompierre.
- **Terre-de-Bas**: Quiet, untouched by large-scale tourism, famous for pottery crafts and lush hiking trails.

## 2. Marie-Galante: The Island of a Hundred Windmills
Known affectionately as "La Grande Galette", Marie-Galante moves to an authentic, unhurried Creole rhythm. Visit historic 59° agricultural rum distilleries (Bielle, Poisson/Père Labat, Bellevue) and relax on pristine beaches like Anse Canot.

## 3. La Désirade: Rugged Solitude
A geological reserve marked by arid plateaus, rare endemic iguanas, and crystalline barrier reefs, accessible via daily boat transfers from Saint-François.
