# Hiking La Soufrière Volcano & National Park: Essential Trail Guide & Safety

Rising to 1,467 meters above the Caribbean Sea, La Soufrière ("La Vieille Dame") is an active stratovolcano nestled within the protected 30,000-hectare Guadeloupe National Park.

---

## 1. Summit Ascent Route (Chemin des Dames)
- **Starting Point**: Bains Jaunes parking lot (elevation 950m), where natural sulfur hot thermal pools await weary hikers on their return.
- **Duration**: Approximately 3.5 to 4.5 hours round-trip.
- **Difficulty**: Moderate to demanding, with rocky scrambles near the summit plateau.
- **Highlights**: Passing through humid cloud forest, climbing the volcanic dome, and observing steaming fumaroles venting sulfuric vapors.

## 2. Essential Safety Rules
1. **Weather Caution**: Summit conditions can change within minutes. Check the volcanic observatory (OVSG-IPGP) bulletin before departure.
2. **Proper Footwear**: Sturdy hiking boots with vibram soles are mandatory; volcanic trails are frequently wet and slippery.
3. **Protection**: Carry windproof rain jackets and sufficient drinking water (at least 2 liters per person).
