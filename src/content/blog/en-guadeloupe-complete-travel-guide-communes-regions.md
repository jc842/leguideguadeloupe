# Guadeloupe Island Complete Guide: Grande-Terre, Basse-Terre & The 32 Communes

Shaped like a giant butterfly moored in the heart of the Lesser Antilles, Guadeloupe offers an extraordinary duality between its two main wings: limestone Grande-Terre and volcanic Basse-Terre, separated only by the slender Rivière Salée mangrove channel.

---

## 1. Grande-Terre: Coral Lagoons, Sugar Plains & Coastal Cliffs
Grande-Terre is the sun-drenched, flatter wing characterized by turquoise lagoons, white coral sand beaches, and historic sugarcane plantations:
- **Le Gosier & Sainte-Anne**: The epicenter of beach life, featuring postcard-perfect spots like Plage de la Caravelle and the uninhabited islet of Gosier.
- **Saint-François & Pointe des Châteaux**: The easternmost peninsula, where jagged limestone cliffs face the roaring Atlantic swell.
- **Le Moule & Anse-Bertrand**: Surfing breaks, dramatic rocky seascapes, and the spectacular cliff formations of Porte d'Enfer.

## 2. Basse-Terre: Lush Rainforest, Cascades & Volcano
Dominated by the active La Soufrière volcano (1,467m), Basse-Terre is a green paradise of primary rainforest, cascading waterfalls, and black sand beaches:
- **Bouillante & Malendure**: Marine sanctuary of the Cousteau Reserve, world-renowned for sea turtle snorkeling and scuba diving.
- **Deshaies**: Charming fishing village famous for botanical gardens, Grand Anse beach, and colorful Creole architecture.
- **Trois-Rivières & Saint-Claude**: Foothills of the volcano offering Amerindian petroglyphs and direct trail access to La Soufrière.

## 3. Practical Travel Recommendations
- **Transportation**: Renting a car at Pointe-à-Pitre Pôle Caraïbes Airport is essential for discovering the island at your own rhythm.
- **Seasonality**: The dry season (*Carême*) extends from December to May with average temperatures around 28°C and lower humidity.
