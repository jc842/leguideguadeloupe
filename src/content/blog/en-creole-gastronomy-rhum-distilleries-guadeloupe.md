# Creole Gastronomy & Rhum Agricole: Culinary Traditions of Guadeloupe

Guadeloupean cuisine is a rich, flavorful synthesis of Amerindian, African, French, and South Asian (Indian) influences refined over centuries.

---

## 1. Iconic Creole Dishes
- **Accras de Morue**: Light, crispy saltfish fritters spiced with Scotch bonnet pepper, scallions, and fresh lime.
- **Colombo de Cabri**: Fragrant goat curry slow-cooked with roasted spices, coriander, mustard seeds, and local sweet potatoes.
- **Bokit**: The quintessential Guadeloupean street food — fried unleavened dough stuffed with shredded chicken, saltfish, or cheese and fiery Creole sauce.
- **Boudin Créole**: Spiced black pudding delicately seasoned with cinnamon, chili, and Caribbean herbs.

## 2. Pure Cane Rhum Agricole Distilleries
Unlike molasses-based industrial rums, Guadeloupean rum is pressed directly from fresh sugar cane juice:
- **Distillerie Damoiseau** (Le Moule): Famous for oak-aged rums and traditional open cane crushing.
- **Distillerie Bologne** (Basse-Terre): Situated at the foot of La Soufrière, renowned for black cane terroir rums.
- **Domaine de Séverin & Reimonenq** (Sainte-Rose): Historic estates showcasing vintage steam-powered cane mills.
