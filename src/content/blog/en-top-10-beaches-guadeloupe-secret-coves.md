# Top 10 Pristine Beaches in Guadeloupe: From Golden Sands to Wild Atlantic Coves

With over 250 kilometers of diverse coastline, Guadeloupe boasts an exceptional variety of beaches ranging from calm barrier reef lagoons to dramatic golden and black volcanic shores.

---

## 1. Plage de Grande Anse (Deshaies)
Spanning over one kilometer of golden volcanic sand bordered by rolling green hills, Grande Anse is widely celebrated as one of the Caribbean's most magnificent beaches. Sunset views here are legendary.

## 2. Plage de la Caravelle (Sainte-Anne)
Protected by a natural coral barrier, this shallow, warm lagoon offers translucent waters, coconut palm shades, and gentle trade winds ideal for stand-up paddleboarding and family bathing.

## 3. Plage de Bois-Jolan (Sainte-Anne)
A tranquil, wilder alternative with powdery white sand and knee-deep turquoise pools extending hundreds of meters out into the lagoon, shaded by sea grape trees.

## 4. Plage de Petite Anse (Bouillante)
A serene cove tucked on the leeward Caribbean coast, known for tranquil waters and rich marine life just a few meters from the shore.

## 5. Plage du Souffleur (Port-Louis)
Facing the Grand Cul-de-Sac Marin, this long stretch of calm turquoise water features a vibrant local Creole picnic atmosphere and splendid snorkeling along shallow coral formations.
